# Governance Structure

## Organizational Hierarchy

### Executive Level
- **CTO**: Strategic decisions, vendor relationships, compliance
- **VP Engineering**: Resource allocation, team management, metrics

### Management Level
- **Director of Operations**: Day-to-day operations, budget, SLA/SLO targets
- **Engineering Manager**: Team development, hiring, skill growth

### Individual Contributor Level
- **Senior Engineers**: Mentorship, architecture decisions, critical changes
- **Software Engineers**: Feature development, bug fixes
- **DevOps Engineers**: Infrastructure, automation, operations

## Decision Authority Matrix

| Decision | Authority | Approval? | Timeline |
|----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Feature development | PM | Yes | Monthly planning |
| Infrastructure change (standard) | Team Lead | Yes | Same day |
| Infrastructure change (critical) | Director | Yes | 4 hours |
| Budget increase | VP/CTO | Yes | 2 weeks |
| Emergency security patch | Duty Manager | Verbal | Immediate |
| New hire | Director | Yes | Approved |
| Vendor selection | Director + CTO | Yes | 2 weeks |
| Policy changes | CTO | Yes | 1 week |

## Communication Channels

### Daily
- Slack #operations for alerts and brief updates
- Email for formal decisions

### Weekly
- Operations review meeting (Mondays 9 AM)
- Architecture sync (Thursdays 10 AM)

### Monthly
- All-hands meeting (last Friday)
- SLA/SLO review (first Friday)

### Quarterly
- Business review (board presentation)
- Strategic planning session
