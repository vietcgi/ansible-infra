# Communication Channels & Procedures

## Channels by Purpose

### #operations (Slack)
- **Purpose**: Real-time operational updates
- **Users**: Operations team, on-call
- **Frequency**: Multiple times daily during incidents
- **Notification**: Yes (critical)

### #incidents (Slack)
- **Purpose**: Active incident tracking
- **Users**: All engineering
- **Frequency**: During incidents
- **Notification**: Yes (immediately on critical)

### #announcements (Slack)
- **Purpose**: Major announcements, maintenance windows
- **Users**: All staff
- **Frequency**: Weekly or as needed
- **Notification**: Yes (scheduled)

### #general (Slack)
- **Purpose**: Team discussions, casual
- **Users**: Everyone
- **Frequency**: Daily
- **Notification**: No

### ops@example.com (Email)
- **Purpose**: Formal documentation, escalation
- **Users**: Operations team
- **Frequency**: As needed
- **Response Time**: Within 4 hours

### incidents@example.com (Email)
- **Purpose**: Post-incident reports, audit trail
- **Users**: Engineering team
- **Frequency**: After incidents
- **Response Time**: Within 24 hours

## Message Format

**For incidents**: Include severity, impact, and ETA
**For announcements**: Include date, duration, impact
**For requests**: Include justification and deadline

## Response Expectations

- **Critical alerts**: Acknowledge within 5 minutes
- **High-priority**: Response within 30 minutes
- **Normal**: Response within 4 hours
- **Low-priority**: Response within 24 hours
