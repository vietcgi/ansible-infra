# Escalation Procedures

## Incident Escalation Path

```
Level 1: On-Call Engineer (immediate response)
  ↓ (if not resolved in 30 min)
Level 2: Team Lead + Senior Engineer
  ↓ (if not resolved in 1 hour)
Level 3: Director of Operations
  ↓ (if not resolved in 2 hours)
Level 4: CTO + Executive Team
```

## Change Request Escalation

```
Standard Change: Team Lead approval
  ↓ (if concerns)
Critical Change: Director approval
  ↓ (if requires investment)
Strategic Change: CTO + VP Engineering
```

## Urgent Decision Path

**For immediate decisions needed**:
1. Page appropriate manager
2. Provide context in 2-3 sentences
3. Await decision
4. Document decision and rationale
5. Follow up with email confirmation

**Expected response times**:
- On-call: < 5 minutes
- Team Lead: < 15 minutes
- Director: < 30 minutes
- CTO: < 1 hour
