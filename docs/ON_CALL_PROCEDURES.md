# On-Call Procedures

## On-Call Schedule

**Weekly rotation**: Monday - Sunday
**Handoff**: 9 AM Monday morning
**Coverage**: 24/7 during assignment

## Responsibilities

### Before Your Week
- Review runbooks and recent incidents
- Ensure pager is working and charged
- Verify VPN access
- Check emergency contact list updated

### During Your Week
- Respond to alerts within 5 minutes
- Acknowledge receipt in Slack
- Provide status updates every 15 minutes for active incident
- Escalate if needed
- Document in incident ticket

### After Your Week
- Handoff to next on-call engineer
- Document any issues that occurred
- Update runbooks based on experience
- Attend post-mortem if incidents occurred

## Compensation

- On-call pay: [X% of base salary] during assignment
- Night/weekend incident: [Y additional] for each incident
- Emergency callout: Minimum 2 hours pay

## Time Off

- Request off-call at least 2 weeks in advance
- Swap with another engineer if needed
- Director approval required
